HOW TO SURVIVE THE NIGHT SHIFT

The office

Mouse to the screen edge to look left / right.

Q / E — left / right door   A / D — hall lights   W — vent shutter

Or click the wall buttons and the yellow desk button. Closed doors and lights drain power. Run out and everything opens.

Cameras

Space (or hover the bottom bar) to raise / lower the monitor. 1–9 or click the map to switch cameras.

You can't use doors or lights while the monitor is up.

Controller / phone

Controller: left stick looks, LB / RB doors, LT / RT lights, Y vent, A cameras, X meds, View phone, Start pause. On the monitor: LB / RB switch camera, X feed, Y music, RT medicine.

Phone: drag to look around, tap the buttons along the bottom, tap the phone box to answer.

Patients

Each patient's STRESS rises all night. At 100 they turn and come for you.

F feed · R calming music · T give medicine — on that patient's camera.

M opens the med station: read the chart, mix the 3 vials it prescribes, load the syringe, then DOSE them on camera. Wrong mix makes it worse.

Read the charts. Some patients like things others hate.

Phone

P to answer / hang up. Owners give hints. Some callers aren't people.
